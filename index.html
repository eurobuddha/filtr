filtr

A free, real-time retro effects studio for images, video, GIFs and your webcam. Turn a photo into ASCII art, a dithered print, a halftone or a CRT/VHS screen grab — then export it as PNG, JPG, animated GIF, video, SVG or plain text. Fifteen effects with composable bloom, grain, scanlines and chromatic aberration, plus retro palettes from Game Boy to Risograph. Everything runs on your device with WebGL2; your images are never uploaded.
